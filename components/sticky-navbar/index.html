Welcome to our Website

A website is often the first impression a potential client has of a business — before a phone call, before a meeting, before a single word is exchanged. A sticky navigation bar like the one above keeps the essentials (brand, main sections, contact) within reach no matter how far a visitor scrolls, which matters most on long pages: portfolios, service catalogs, blogs. Small details like this rarely get noticed when they work well, but they are missed immediately when they are gone.

Keep scrolling and watch the bar above switch to its dark state — a small visual cue that tells the visitor they have moved past the hero section and into the page content. Effects like this one are pure CSS and JavaScript, no frameworks required, which keeps the page fast and easy to drop into an existing site.
